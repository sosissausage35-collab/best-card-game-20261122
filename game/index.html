EMBER DUEL

A duel of ember, tide, gale & stone.
Outplay the Cinder Warden in 3 rounds.

How to play

  • Each turn you gain mana (🔷) and draw a card.
  • Cards cost mana: Ember burns, Tide heals, Gale draws, Stone shields.
  • Reduce the Warden to 0 ♥ to win the round. Win all 3 rounds to win the duel — the Warden grows stronger each round.
  • Shield 🛡️ absorbs damage first. Your deck reshuffles when it runs out.

Mouse + keyboard · no network needed · best score is saved on this device
